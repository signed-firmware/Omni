Omnis shredders (also known as Omnis also known as Garbage also known as Omni Bugs) are a shredder variant that usually have 4-6 Arms that are made up of engines and blades, along with a structure. These are typically more annoying to build due to having to manually copy and paste the same arm multiple times. They also have very high block counts compared to other shredders.
