Early development

from information from obl 2, shredders are assumed to be derived from missiles missiles were mouse aimed and used weapons and they probably tried using cutters, then developed the og (shredder bug) missiles then developed into warpers, the shredder bug ogs developed into actual ogs with 2 motors on each axis omnis are either developed alongside ogs or after, some warpers are seen with similar weapon structure to omnis so im not sure but the first pvp builds we have been shown by truth are missiles but with more wings and rockets with weapons added

ogs, omnis and warpers developed, warp league and csa created and obl was retired csa developed a gap in skill between them and other players, these are gen 3 shredders
