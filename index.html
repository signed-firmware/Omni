Quick start

three steps to your first working shredder

Understand massless

Massless is the core function of every shredder — without it, your blades won't spin right. Start with the Massless & Staging guide.

Pick your core

Gyro cores, OGs, balljoints and omnis are the four main shredder types. Read up on Gyro Cores to learn what fits your build.

Armor it, then shred

Blades do the cutting, but armor decides who survives. Learn pushback, thickness and angling before you take it to PvP.
